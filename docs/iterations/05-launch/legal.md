## Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Ocean Above is a demonstration flight-booking assistant operated by {{OPERATOR}}. It is not a real airline: no flights
operate, no payments are taken and tickets have no value.

**What we collect**
- If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address and name, as provided by Google Sign-In. We do not
  receive your password and request no other Google data or permissions.
- If you sign in with an invite code: the name you enter.
- What you do in the app: your messages, the assistant's replies, quotes and the demo tickets you book.
- Technical data needed to run the service: request logs (including IP address) kept for up to 14 days, and a session
  cookie that keeps you signed in.

**How we use it**
- To run the assistant, show your tickets and your conversation history, and enforce usage limits.
- Your messages are sent to our language-model provider (DeepSeek) to generate replies. Do not enter sensitive personal
  information.
- We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

**Retention and deletion**
- Data is kept while the demo runs. Email {{CONTACT}} to have your account and data deleted.
